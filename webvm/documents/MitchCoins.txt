========================================
             MITCHCOIN SYSTEM
========================================
MitchCoins (M$) are the official currency of mitch.pro.
You can earn them by:
- Referrals (2,000 MitchCoins each!)
- Solving Security Challenges / CTFs (run `htb` in main console)
- Solving Chess Puzzles (in Chess Bot page)
- Daily login streaks (60 consecutive days grants premium!)
- Selling items on the Marketplace

Use them at the Shop to unlock awesome items, or trade them with friends!
