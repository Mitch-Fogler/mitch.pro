Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers about accounts, privacy, and gaming.

Is my chat activity actually private?
Yes. Our chatrooms utilize client-side hashing and end-to-end encrypted tunnels. Conversations are transient and designed to disappear, meaning they are never stored or logged on any remote server database.
What is MitchSync?
MitchSync is our proprietary background API that syncs your local browser state with your profile token. It saves data like emulator game progress, statistics, settings, and high scores automatically.
What are Mitch Coins?
Mitch Coins are a site-wide virtual currency. You earn them by placing pixels on the global canvas, solving daily chess puzzles, winning arcade games, or participating in events. They have zero real-world value and cannot be purchased with real money.
What is "Premium" status?
Premium is a cosmetic upgrade that unlocks perks like Canvas Protection (locking your pixels so they can't be overwritten), custom badges, rainbow name animation in chat and leaderboards, and access to a private, premium-only chat room.
How does E2E Encryption work here?
Direct DMs use End-to-End Encryption (E2E). This means your messages are scrambled on your device and only the recipient can unscramble them. Even mitch.pro administrators cannot read your private messages. We use the browser's native Web Crypto API (AES-GCM 256-bit) for maximum security.
