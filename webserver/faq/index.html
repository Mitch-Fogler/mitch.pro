The Basics

What is mitch.pro?

mitch.pro is a private community platform for students to hang out, play games, create art on a shared canvas, and communicate securely. It features E2E encrypted chat, a competitive global leaderboard, and a virtual economy built around interaction.

How do I get access?

Access is restricted to verified students. You must submit your student email on the enroll page. Once your status is verified (manually or automatically), you will receive an invitation to set up your account password and E2E keys.

What are Mitch Coins?

Mitch Coins are a site-wide virtual currency. You earn them by:
• Placing pixels on the global canvas.
• Solving daily chess puzzles.
• Winning arcade games or chess matches.
• Participating in community events.
They have zero real-world value and cannot be purchased with real money.

Account & Security

How do I change my password?

To change your password, go to the Enrollment Page and enter your email. After verifying with the 6-digit OTP sent to your inbox, you will be prompted to set a new password. This will also re-derive your E2E encryption keys.

What happens if I lose my login or token?

Since we don't store your raw password (only a secure hash), you can reset it anytime via the enrollment flow. If you lose access to your student email entirely, you will need to contact support@mitch.pro for manual identity verification.

Can I have multiple accounts?

No. To maintain fairness on the leaderboard and canvas, we strictly enforce a one-account-per-student policy. Attempting to bypass this via alternate emails may lead to a permanent ban of all associated accounts.

Is there a mobile app?

mitch.pro is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA). On iPhone, tap "Share" then "Add to Home Screen". On Android, tap the three dots then "Install App". This gives you a full-screen experience and push notifications without needing an app store.

Privacy & Data

Is my student email shared with anyone?

No. Your real email address is masked throughout the public UI (e.g., yourname@student.mitch.pro). Only administrators can see your original student email for account management and security verification.

What is E2E Encryption and how does it work here?

Direct DMs use End-to-End Encryption (E2E). This means your messages are scrambled on your device and only the recipient can unscramble them. Even mitch.pro administrators cannot read your private messages. We use the browser's native Web Crypto API (AES-GCM 256-bit) for maximum security.

What data do you collect?

We only log the bare minimum: your email (for auth), your hashed password, and your site activity (pixels placed, game scores, coin balance). We temporarily log IP addresses for 48 hours strictly to prevent DDoS attacks and bot abuse.

Does mitch.pro track my searches or AI queries?

No. AI queries are processed in real-time. We do not maintain a permanent history of your queries tied to your profile, and we do not use your data to train models or show advertisements.

How do I delete my account and data?

You have full ownership of your data. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data (profile, coins, stats, and E2E keys) directly from your Profile Settings. Once you confirm deletion, your data is instantly and permanently purged from our active databases. If you cannot access your account, email support@mitch.pro for manual assistance.

Community & Perks

What is "Premium" status?

Premium is a cosmetic upgrade that unlocks perks like:
• Canvas Protection: Lock your pixels so they can't be overwritten.
• Custom Badges: Display unique icons next to your name.
• Rainbow Names: Animated name colors in chat and leaderboards.
• The Sanctum: Access to a private, premium-only chat room.

How do I report a player or a bug?

To report a bug, use the /bug command in any chat or email us. To report harassment in DMs, use the "Report" button inside the conversation; this will securely share a transcript of the recent messages with the moderation team for review.

Can I gift my Mitch Coins to friends?

Yes! You can use the /gift command in chat or visit a friend's profile to transfer coins. Note that there is a small "tax" on transfers to prevent economy inflation.

How do I join the site staff?

We periodically look for active, helpful members to join our moderation and development teams. If you have coding skills or community management experience, check the Join the Team page for open roles.
